Initial public submission of Vibe Catalysis. The skills-only plugin builds elemental fcc, bcc, and hcp metal surfaces with ASE, enumerates surface-specific adsorption sites, preserves fixed slab layers, runs local FAIR-Chem UMA single-point and relaxation calculations, screens geometries, ranks accepted candidates, and creates energy plots with ASE-native top views. Users must separately install the Python dependencies, obtain access to the gated facebook/UMA model, and authenticate with Hugging Face. No credentials or model weights are included.
